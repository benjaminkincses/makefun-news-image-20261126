# Poster stories

**CAIRO TANKER FIRE KILLS 31**
A fuel tanker overturned and burst into flames on a Cairo ring road in Warraq, killing at least 31 people and injuring 21.

**TRUMP PROPOSES TARIFFS ON 14 PARTNERS**
The Trump administration unveiled proposed tariffs on 14 economies, among them China, the European Union, Japan and India.

**A MILLION SATELLITES GET THE GREEN LIGHT**
The FCC authorized SpaceX to deploy a constellation of up to one million satellites to serve as data centres in orbit.

**JUDGES URGE DROPPING NETANYAHU CASE**
Israeli judges recommended that prosecutors drop the Case 4000 bribery case against Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

**UKRAINE SAYS FIVE RUSSIAN JETS HIT**
Ukrainian forces said a long-range strike damaged five aircraft at the Savasleyka airbase east of Moscow.

**HOSTAGES FREED AFTER 17-HOUR SIEGE**
Colombian security forces stormed a supermarket in Cartagena and rescued the hostages held there for 17 hours.

**KIM ORDERS MORE TACTICAL NUKES**
Kim Jong Un called for a wider deployment of tactical nuclear weapons during an inspection of nuclear material production facilities.

**US CONSORTIUM TO BUY LUKOIL ASSETS**
An American-led consortium agreed to acquire LUKOIL International's global energy assets.
